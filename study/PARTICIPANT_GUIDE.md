# Calorie Lab: participant instructions

Thank you for considering this 15–20 minute session. We are testing whether an AI research demo is clear and usable. We are testing the interface, not your ability; confusion and unsuccessful tasks are useful feedback.

Please take part only if you are 18 or older, comfortable reading English, and have not previously used or helped develop this demo. You will use fictional examples only. Do not enter your own body measurements, exercise history or health information.

Participation is optional. You can skip a question, stop or ask to withdraw your session data. We will not record audio/video. The moderator will record task outcomes, approximate times, answers and paraphrased comments under a participant code; no name, email or health data goes in the study sheet. Scheduling contact is kept separately. Only anonymized aggregate results and paraphrased usability issues may be used in the public portfolio. Private response notes will be deleted within 30 days after the aggregate report is finalized. You can ask to remove your coded session before aggregation; already published aggregate facts may not be retractable individually.

Before starting, tell the moderator whether you agree to take part and to this use of anonymized findings. You can keep your participant code if you want to request withdrawal. There is no pressure to agree or give positive feedback.

## During the session

Open [Calorie Lab](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Moobakr/calorie-lab-fewer-sensors) when the moderator asks. Please say what you are looking for, thinking and finding confusing. The moderator will usually wait before helping. You may stop or skip any task. These task values describe invented people, not you.

The moderator will tell you the task order. Open a fresh copy of the app before tasks 1 and 2.

**Task 1.** A fictional session has these available measurements: recorded gender female, age 42 years, height 172 cm, weight 70 kg, duration 20 minutes and heart rate 105 bpm. Temperature was not measured. Using all the available measurements, show the estimated recorded calories and the interval. Do not invent a temperature reading. Tell the moderator when you believe you have finished.

**Task 2.** For another fictional session, you know recorded gender male, age 60 years, height 180 cm, weight 85 kg and duration 28 minutes. Neither heart rate nor temperature was measured. Using all the available measurements, obtain an estimate and interval without inventing readings. Tell the moderator when you believe you have finished.

**Task 3.** Look at the reference interval and the explanation on the page. In your own words, what does “90%” describe? What, if anything, could you conclude about one person's actual calories burned from this output? You may inspect any explanatory text on the page.

**Task 4.** Explore the benchmark comparison and explanations. What would you tell a visitor about leaving out temperature, or leaving out both heart rate and temperature? Which session-length group, if any, needs extra caution about the interval, and why?

## Feedback after the tasks

You can leave any answer blank.

1. How easy was it to obtain the estimates? **1 very difficult · 2 difficult · 3 neither · 4 easy · 5 very easy**
2. How clear were the explanations of uncertainty and limitations? **1 very unclear · 2 unclear · 3 neither · 4 clear · 5 very clear**
3. How useful was this demo for understanding the project's measurement/error tradeoff? **1 not useful at all · 2 slightly useful · 3 moderately useful · 4 useful · 5 very useful**
4. What was most confusing or misleading?
5. What one change would improve it?

These are custom feedback questions, not a standardized usability test.
