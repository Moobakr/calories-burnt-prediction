## Authorship record

Confirmed author actions in this collaboration: provided the original notebook and CSV, set the portfolio/scholarship objective, authorized the research workflow, chose the free Static hosting route and English study materials, completed GitHub/Hugging Face authentication steps in the user's session, confirmed basic live-demo interaction and chose to skip participant testing.

The AI assistant performed substantial implementation and tool-assisted execution: repository restructuring, protocols, reproducibility tooling, fitting/evaluation runners, verification, model export/browser code, documentation and application drafts. Dataset observations originated outside this collaboration. The author should distinguish project ownership from independently writing or validating every component. No claim is made here that the author independently designed the statistics, wrote the implementation without assistance, collected the underlying data, or personally reran every check.

Suggested disclosure: “This project was developed with substantial AI-assistant support for implementation, analysis tooling and documentation. I supplied the starting materials, directed the project choices, completed account setup and performed a basic manual demo check. The repository records the evaluation and verification evidence.” Expand your personal contribution only for work you actually perform and can substantiate. Follow the selected application's actual disclosure instructions rather than assuming one policy applies everywhere.

## Outcomes versus impact

Verified outputs are source, reports, trained frozen systems, reliability findings, software checks and a public demo. The measured research outcome is error/coverage on recorded labels. The usability study was skipped; there are no observed user-performance or benefit results. Download counts, visitors, health improvements, money saved, time saved, publication citations and admissions outcomes have not been measured. Do not convert an output or proposed future use into an impact claim.

No novel estimator, priority claim, publication, award, production adoption or clinical approval is asserted. A separate documented-data replication is a future research direction, not a completed result.
